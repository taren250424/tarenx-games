ESDB carries its own upstream credits, reproduced here in full:

ESDB is derived from many sources, most of which are in the Public Domain. Data from the Corpus of Contemporary American English (COCA) was also used.

All data from COCA comes from 3-gram data that is not freely available; however, the usage is within the rights given by the NDA that was signed when purchasing the data. More information on COCA is available at english-corpora.org/coca.

The primary source of words for ESDB comes from 12dicts and ENABLE2K. Both are in the Public Domain, but Alan Beale <biljir@pobox.com> deserves special credit as he is the author of 12dicts and a major contributor to ENABLE2K. In addition, he gave me an incredible amount of feedback and created a number of special lists in order to help improve the overall quality of ESDB.

Sokoban — Microban level collection

The Microban levels are the collection by David W. Skinner, released for free distribution with attribution. Source.

Passant — positions

Positions come from the Lichess puzzle database, released by Lichess under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication. Each record keeps the puzzle id, position, rating and themes from that database.

The move evaluations were computed ahead of time with Stockfish (GPL-3.0). Stockfish is not part of this site; the evaluations it produced are plain data.

The chess piece set, board and all other Passant artwork were drawn for this site.

Passant — chess rules

Legal moves, check and move notation come from chess.js, which is included in the game's script.
