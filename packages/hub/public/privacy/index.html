Privacy Policy

This policy covers games.tarenx.com. The short version: every game here runs entirely in your browser, we run no servers of our own, and no accounts or sign-ups are involved.

Local storage

Games save progress, best scores, and preferences (such as sound settings) in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

Analytics

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage (page views, approximate region, device type). These services set cookies. See the Google Privacy Policy for details, or opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use advertising cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Third-party vendors' use of advertising cookies is described in How Google uses cookies in advertising.

Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent message before any advertising cookies are set.
